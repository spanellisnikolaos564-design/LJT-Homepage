I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am pursuing a **Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024 - Present)** under the supervision of **Professor Junxian He**. I received my **B.Eng.** degree from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024, having studied there from 2020 to 2024, where I was previously advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies. During my undergraduate studies I received the **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship** from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**.

## Research Interests

* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
